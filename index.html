The Gap

You want to grow beyond referrals.

About Me

Hi, I'm Niam.

I grew up in a family-run opticians on the high street in Watford. My mum still runs it. That shop taught me everything I know about small businesses: staff leaving unexpectedly, the endless tasks nobody wants to do, the work that makes you question why you started in the first place.

Since then I've worked in sales, spent years in data analytics (Python, dashboards, the lot), led teams at a children's charity for over a decade, and coached people through fitness transformations off the back of my own 50kg weight loss. On paper it looks scattered. In practice, it's been the perfect training ground for what I do now.

I work with small UK accountancy practices because I see the same pattern there as in every small business I've been close to. The work that eats your team's day, chasing documents, re-keying receipts, answering the same client questions every quarter, isn't the work that grew the practice. And it's not the work that will grow it from here.

I help practice owners build the systems that take that work off the team's plate. Lead generation, post-enquiry follow-up, document chasing, late-payment reminders, repetitive client communications. Done for you. Built around how your practice actually runs.

The goal isn't just efficiency. It's freedom. More time with your family. More headspace to work on the practice instead of in it. Fewer Sunday nights on a spreadsheet.
